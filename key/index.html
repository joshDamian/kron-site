Get an AI key for Lumen

A couple of minutes, and Lumen is ready.

Lumen runs on an AI provider you pick. You pay that provider directly, only for what Lumen uses, and your key stays in your phone's secure storage.

Which provider do you use?

Step 1

Make an account

Sign up at the Anthropic console, then add a little credit under Billing.

Open platform.claude.com

Step 2

Create your key

Go to API keys → Create key, then copy it. You'll paste it into Kron in the last step.

Open your API keys

Step 3

Paste it into Kron

In Kron: Settings → Lumen → Anthropic → paste the key. That's it — Lumen's ready.

Keep the cost in check

Set a monthly spend limit in your provider's billing settings. Closing a day is a small call. Chat and dashboards over long stretches cost more. In Settings → Lumen → Thinking, "Low" keeps costs down with OpenAI-compatible providers.
